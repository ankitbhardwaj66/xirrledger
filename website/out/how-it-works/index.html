How It Works

Calculate your portfolio XIRR in 4 simple steps. Takes less than 5 minutes.

Download Your Ledger

Export your trading ledger from your broker's website. For Zerodha, download the CSV from Funds → View Statement. For Groww, download PDF statements for each year.

✦ Tip: Select the full date range — from your first investment till today — for accurate results.

Upload Files

Upload your ledger files to the calculator. You can upload multiple files at once — from the same broker or different brokers. Files from the same account (PAN) are automatically combined.

✦ Your files are processed securely and never stored on our servers.

Enter Current Values

Input your current portfolio values — holdings value and available cash. Separate input fields appear for each account if you have multiple.

✦ You can find these values on your broker's app or website dashboard.

Get Your XIRR

View detailed analysis with XIRR calculation, Nifty 50 benchmark comparison, and all key metrics. Download a professional PDF report for your records or tax filing.

✦ PDF reports are perfect for sharing with financial advisors or CAs.

2

3

Select All Segments as category

4

Set date range (first investment till today)

5

Click the blue arrow → then click the CSV link

File type: CSV

Groww

PDF Format

1

Log in to Groww

2

Go to Funds → All Transactions

3

Select date & year (max 1 year per PDF)

4

Click Download

5

Repeat for all years from first investment till today

File type: PDF

Password: Your PAN (uppercase)

Download one PDF per year for the full period
