Current Features

Core Feature

Ledger-Based Calculation

The only calculator that works directly with your broker ledger files. Upload CSV or PDF files for 100% accurate XIRR calculations without manual data entry.

Popular

Multi-Broker Support

Currently supports Zerodha (CSV) and Groww (PDF). Combine ledgers from different brokers for consolidated portfolio analysis.

Multi-Account Analysis

Manage multiple trading accounts effortlessly. Get individual XIRR for each account plus combined portfolio metrics in one comprehensive report.

Essential

Nifty 50 Benchmark

Automatically compare your portfolio performance against Nifty 50 index. See if you're beating the market with detailed comparison metrics.

PDF Report Generation

Download professional PDF reports with all metrics, perfect for tax filing, financial advisors, or personal record keeping.

Comprehensive Metrics

Track total invested, withdrawn, current value, net gain/loss, simple returns, annualized XIRR, and investment period - all in one place.

Privacy & Security

All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your financial data never leaves your device. No registration or data storage required.

Automatic Transaction Detection

Smart parsing of your ledger files automatically identifies deposits, withdrawals, payouts, and quarterly settlements.

Password-Protected PDFs

Support for password-protected Groww PDFs. Securely process encrypted files with your PAN number as password.
