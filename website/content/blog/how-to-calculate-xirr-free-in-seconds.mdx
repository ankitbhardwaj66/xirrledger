## Why "Free in Seconds" Is Actually Possible

The calculation itself — the XIRR formula — is not complex. The hard part is getting the right data into it.

Most people try to track individual stock trades. That approach misses every rupee you paid in STT, brokerage, exchange charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty, and GST. The result looks right but runs 0.5–1.5% higher than your actual return.

The correct input is your **broker's fund statement** — the ledger that records every actual cash flow in and out of your account, with all charges already reflected.

XIRR Ledger reads that file directly. You don't build a spreadsheet. You just upload the file you already have.

## Step 1: Download Your Broker Ledger

This is the only thing you need to gather. Takes 2–3 minutes.

### Zerodha
1. Log in to [console.zerodha.com](https://console.zerodha.com)
2. Go to **Reports → Fund Statement**
3. Set segment to **All Segments**, pick your date range (start from when you first deposited funds)
4. Click **Download CSV**

### Groww
1. Log in to [groww.in](https://groww.in)
2. Go to **Profile → Reports → Transactions**
3. Select all time, choose **Download**
4. Use the **P&L / ledger statement** (PDF or CSV depending on account type)

### Fyers
1. Log in to [fyers.in](https://fyers.in)
2. Go to **My Account → Reports → Ledger**
3. Set date range from your first deposit to today
4. Download as CSV

If you have **multiple broker accounts**, download a ledger from each. XIRR Ledger handles all of them together — you don't need to merge anything yourself.
